Klondike Solitaire

• Click Stock to flip a card to Waste
• Click a card to select it (yellow glow), then click a valid destination
• Build tableau in alternating color, descending order
• Move Aces to Foundations first; build up by suit to King
• Win when all 52 cards reach the 4 foundations!
